Cerakote Laser Color Guide

Your 77 laser colors with a sampled swatch from each color's Cerakote.com product page, Cerakote's own laser-imaging recommendations, and an estimate of how well each one reads when the laser cuts through to bare metal.

How to read this

Three ways to laser Cerakote

  • Ablate to metal: remove the coating to expose the substrate.
  • Two-layer: remove a top coat to reveal a base coat of another color.
  • Laser imaging: shift the tone of a single color without removing it, so the protection stays intact.

Imaging list Tone-shift colors

Colors from your list that Cerakote names as recommended for laser imaging (24 of 77). Use them for ghost logos, camo, topo and pattern work. Cerakote recommends a 20–60 W fiber laser and a power-vs-frequency "square test" on 3"×6" panels, starting around 500–800 kHz.

Metal contrast rating

High fine lines, small text and halftones read well. Medium keep line weights bold and skip hairlines. Low the cut blends with bare metal, so use big filled shapes, color-fill, or a two-layer job instead.

Caveats

Hex values are sampled from Cerakote's product photos (swatch panels for H-Series, the coated tumbler for F-Series), so they are close but lit. The contrast rating compares each swatch to a mid-grey "bare metal" tone (#B0B0B0). Your substrate (aluminum, nitrided steel, polymer) will change the real result, so confirm on a test panel before quoting a job.
